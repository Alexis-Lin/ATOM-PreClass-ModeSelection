Why restructure

Today’s three cards imply Live Coach and Record & Recap are separate, locked choices. They’re actually one AI mode with coaching on/off — and switchable mid-session. The menu should say that.

A vs B vs C

A smallest change, switch inline. B clearest “two modes” model, best if Manual grows. C most explicit that it’s one thing, but the card feels heavier.

Manual, two sub-modes

Self-paced — confirm each move to advance (today). Timed class — paced, guided, like Fitness+/Ladder (Coming soon).
